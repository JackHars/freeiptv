Connect your IPTV app.

Use this account to browse movies, TV series, and sports events from another app on your home network.

XTREAM CODES · HLS

1 Xtream Codes login

  1. In your IPTV app, add an Xtream Codes account.
  2. Enter the server, username, and password below.
  3. Choose HLS / m3u8 if it asks for a format, then open Movies, Series, or Live TV.

Account name can be anything, for example “Cinejoy Home”. Your phone or TV needs to be on the same LAN as this server.

2 Or use an M3U playlist

For apps that accept a playlist URL. Includes sports events, the movie catalog, and all episodes of series you pin below or add from the cinema.
